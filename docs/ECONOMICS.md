# Linked by Light — Launch economics (hypotheses, not actuals)

**Date:** 2026-11-17. This is a planning model with *illustrative* prices/fees, not a prediction. All currency amounts USD unless noted. Source current marketplace rates and regional taxes from primary platform agreements before launch.

## Proposed product
- Steam list price: **$5.99** one paid copy. Free standalone Steam demo, optional Friend Pass pending store/entitlement design.
- Google Play / App Store: free download, $3.99 full unlock, verified native store billing.
- No ads, subscription or microtransaction store at first release.
- Cross-device room play is core; cross-platform purchase rights are a separate licensing/policy problem.

## Unit economics assumptions
- Steam net of discounted regional mix/refunds: $5.99 × 0.85 × (1−0.30) = **$3.56405** per purchase.
- Mobile net of discounted mix/refunds: $3.99 × 0.85 × (1−0.20) = **$2.7132** per in-app unlock.
- Mobile paying conversion **is not known**; do not assume installs monetize.
- The gross margin estimates exclude corporate income taxes, payment FX, VAT handling, customer support, server variable cost and full unpaid founder labor.
- Platform fee schedules vary by jurisdiction, program and payment model. **Review before release**.

## Example 12-month scenarios
| Scenario | Steam copies | Mobile installs | Mobile paid conversion | Total after assumed deductions | Project expense | Cash result before taxes |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Weak | 200 | 5,000 | 1% | ≈$848 | $5,000 | ≈−$4,152 |
| Base | 1,500 | 30,000 | 2% | ≈$6,974 | $9,000 | ≈−$2,026 |
| Strong | 8,000 | 150,000 | 3% | ≈$40,722 | $20,000 | ≈+$20,722 |

At $9,000 fixed budget with only Steam sales: 9000 / 3.56405 ≈ **2,525.2**, thus 2,526 copies rounded up. Values exclude variable hosting that grows with use.

## Budget tiers
- <$1,000: focused 30-minute demo, 50-pair validation, basic market materials.
- $5,000–12,000: aspirational self-developed Steam release, external QA/art/music budget.
- $3,000–8,000 incremental: Android+iOS packaging, native payments, hardware QA/store compliance.
- Not included: market rates for all engineering hours, legal counsel, high-scale customer acquisition.

## Actual metrics to capture
Acquisition source, wishlists, Steam demo-to-purchase, platform-by-platform paid conversion, refunds, real checkout receipts, gross/net payout, session length, room pairing and server costs / pair-hour. Set campaigns budget caps until margin is known.

## Investment gates
- First commit cap: up to $1k for vertical slice validation (not paid acquisition at scale).
- Next tranche only after first-use G1 and vertical slice G2 evidence.
- Full PC release investment only after G3/G4 with a sellable build and a measured wishlist/demo funnel.
- Android/iOS ports funded only after confirming unit economics and the technical ability to maintain one crossplay ecosystem.
