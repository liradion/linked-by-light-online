Privacy information

Information used to play

To enable cooperative play, the server temporarily processes a room code, random player-seat tokens, character state and actions. Your browser may store a return token, level progress and preferences in localStorage. Do not share room codes or recovery keys with strangers.

When you voluntarily submit feedback, the game processes the report type, text, optional rating, broad device category and technical level number. Please do not include names, phone numbers, email addresses, passwords or room codes. Feedback is only submitted when you check the consent box.

Cloud saves

When cloud storage is configured, game saves and room state may be stored on the server. A recovery key protects access to your cloud profile; losing it may make data inaccessible. Optional Google sign-in is available only when that integration is configured.

Without durable storage, rooms, community maps, leaderboards and feedback may be lost when the server restarts. Export local game saves to preserve important progress.

Community content

A player-created map title may become public only after moderator approval. Private room messages are intended for the two players and must not be disclosed to spectators. Do not put personal information in public titles or feedback.

Diagnostics and retention

The game counts aggregate connections, created rooms, errors and latencies without storing personal identifiers in these aggregated counters. Administrative data requires an operator secret. Up to 100 latest feedback reports are retained in memory; a long-term retention policy must be approved before commercial launch. Hosting providers may separately retain infrastructure logs.

Your choices

Feedback is optional. You can clear local progress using your browser's site-data settings, but this does not delete previously submitted server-side feedback. Until an official support contact is published, use the in-game Feedback form for privacy or removal requests.
