Terms of use

Beta status

Linked by Light is available as a free public beta. Bugs, network interruptions, server downtime and gameplay changes may occur. There are no paid items or automatic charges in this beta.

Shared rooms

A room code acts as an invitation. Share it only with the people you want to play with. Do not disrupt other players, attempt to obtain another person's tokens or overload the service with automated requests.

Player-created maps

You may only publish materials you have the rights to use. Threats, harassment, disclosure of personal information and unlawful content are prohibited. Map titles and level geometry are submitted for moderation. Maps are not available in the shared gallery before approval. Moderators may reject or hide a map; users can report published content.

Progress and support

Game progress may be stored locally and, if configured, in cloud storage. Make your own backups. Use the in-game Feedback button to report issues or ideas. Do not submit someone else's personal information.

Development

Game features may change during testing. Leaderboards, user-created maps and social features may be disabled to address safety issues. Official operator details and digital purchase terms are required before a commercial launch.
